PERSONAL PHILOSOPHY

High Conviction

Concentrated capital allocation in the highest-conviction ideas after deep, multi-year research.

Similar framework to Steven Mark Ryan
•
Multi-decade time horizon

After years of studying markets, businesses, and my own psychology, I arrived at the same conclusion as other high-conviction investors: when you have developed genuine edge through obsessive research, the rational response is not diversification — it is concentration.

I follow a framework very similar to Steven Mark Ryan: identify one (or very few) exceptional opportunities where my understanding is significantly deeper than the market’s, then allocate aggressively and hold for decades through volatility.

“Diversification is a hedge against ignorance. If you know what you’re doing, concentration is the only logical choice.”

— Core principle I share with the high-conviction school

Core Principles

01

Conviction Must Be Earned

I only reach high conviction after thousands of hours across financials, technology, competitive dynamics, management character, and second-order effects. Surface-level understanding is not enough.

02

Concentration Over Diversification

When the probability-weighted upside of one idea dwarfs everything else in your universe, spreading capital dilutes returns. I would rather be 80-100% in my best idea than 5% in twenty good ones.

03

Multi-Decade Time Horizon

True compounding requires patience most investors do not possess. I optimize for 15–30+ year outcomes, not quarterly or even annual performance.

04

Ruthless Measurement & Frugality

I track income, expenses, and capital allocation with extreme granularity (see “My Finances” and “Network Tracker” sheets). Every dollar not spent on lifestyle is capital that can compound in the highest-conviction idea.

Process & Tracking

My two primary private tools are:

  • My Finances — Detailed tracking of income, savings rate, capital deployment, and net worth over time. The goal is radical clarity on how much capital I can actually put to work each year.
  • Network Tracker — Systematic mapping of relationships, information flow, and opportunity sources. In high-conviction investing, who you know and what asymmetric information you can access matters enormously.

This mirrors the discipline I admire in Steven Mark Ryan: obsessive documentation of the inputs (income, savings, research time) that enable the output (concentrated ownership in an exceptional business).

Why This Approach Fits

High-conviction concentration is not for everyone. It requires specific traits I have spent years developing:

• Tolerance for extreme volatility without emotional interference
• Genuine enjoyment of deep, solitary research
• Willingness to look wrong for long periods
• Strong internal scorecard rather than external validation

The same rigor I apply to engineering and theological inquiry — precise definitions, falsifiability, humility before evidence — transfers directly to capital allocation.

Important

This is a personal philosophy, not advice. Concentrated investing can lead to permanent capital loss. I have high risk tolerance, a long time horizon, and (I believe) informational and analytical edge in specific areas. Most people are better served with broad diversification.
