05 · Method and limits

Adaptive pacing, not price prediction.

Traditional dollar-cost averaging invests equal portions at regular intervals. This lab keeps that discipline but lets observable weakness change when a fixed monthly budget is deployed.

1

Fix the cap

Monthly budget × allocation creates an asset target. Contributions already made are deducted first.

2

Find the baseline

The remaining target is divided by remaining Nasdaq trading sessions, including the next eligible session.

3

Weight the day

Daily and weekly returns, the 20-day trend, drawdown, RSI, and volatility produce a bounded multiplier.

4

Respect the month

Strong days retain a floor buy, weak days cannot exceed the ceiling, and the final session catches up the remainder.

Concentration risk

TSLA and SPCX can both be highly volatile and share key-person and technology-sector risks. A pacing rule does not make a concentrated portfolio diversified.

Short SPCX history

SPCX began trading in June 2026. Until 60 sessions exist, the engine pulls its signal toward neutral and caps weak-day sizing at 1.75×.

Data and execution

Nasdaq data may be delayed. Manual price mode improves share estimates intraday, but the actual fill, fees, FX, and taxes remain broker-side.
