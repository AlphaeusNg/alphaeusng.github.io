Workflow
  1. Import a document (DOCX, PDF, TXT, or Markdown).
  2. Scan status, diagnostics, and chapter outline.
  3. Fix structure in Edit (opens by default) or HTML.
  4. Use Diff for full-document track changes; jump from the change index.
  5. Switch to Device, choose your Kobo and reading settings, then page through the converted output.

5. EPUB export

Download EPUB

Download always pulls the latest Edit/HTML body first, then builds the EPUB. Open the Diff tab to review track changes in the full document.

Device specification sources

Profiles use Rakuten Kobo’s published technical specifications for Clara BW, Clara Colour, Libra Colour, Sage, and Elipsa 2E. Screen placement inside the bezel is estimated because Kobo publishes body and display dimensions, not bezel offsets.
