1. Import

Drop a file here

Best results come from DOCX. PDFs are supported with client-side structure reconstruction that tries to preserve line grouping, paragraph breaks, indentation, and table grids.

Why this works better
  • DOCX keeps native paragraph structure, so conversion stays close to the source.
  • PDF extraction groups text by line height and x-position to reconstruct spaces and paragraph blocks.
  • Grid-like PDF regions are detected and rebuilt as HTML tables (column clustering + row proximity).
  • Indented lines stay indented in both the preview and the exported EPUB.
