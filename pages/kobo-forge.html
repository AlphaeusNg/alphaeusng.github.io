Workflow
  1. Import a document (DOCX, PDF, TXT, or Markdown).
  2. Scan status, diagnostics, and chapter outline.
  3. Preview, then fix with Edit or HTML.
  4. Optional: toggle e-ink for paper-like contrast.
  5. Download EPUB at the bottom and sideload to your Kobo.
