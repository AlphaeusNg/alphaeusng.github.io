WHO I AM

The Story

I first got drawn into AI watching systems like AlphaGo and AlphaStar outperform humans at complex games. That curiosity led me into computer science and eventually into building practical AI systems.

Today, I work as an AI Research Engineer at Panasonic, developing internal supply chain automation tools from end to end. Before that, I worked on computer vision systems for threat detection at HTX. Those experiences taught me to value systems that remain useful beyond a prototype or benchmark.

Outside of work, I spend a lot of time reading Scripture, studying theology, and thinking about truth. The same habits that help me debug models also push me to examine ideas and beliefs more carefully.

SUMMARY

I started with inherited belief and a lot of apologetics. Over time, that became a more personal search for truth, with Scripture first, Christ at the center, and enough honesty to admit that some of my favorite frameworks were too small.

That meant wrestling seriously with sovereignty, free will, baptism, church life, and the kind of teaching that produces repentance, joy, unity, and love for Jesus.

The thing that has held steady is simple: salvation is by grace alone, through faith alone, in Jesus alone. If doctrine does not make us more honest, more humble, and more alive to God, something is off.
