WHO I AM

The Story

I got hooked early on systems that could beat humans at their own games. AlphaGo. AlphaStar. Elegant overkill. That rabbit hole dragged me into computer science and then straight into AI.

Now I work at Panasonic as an AI Research Engineer, building internal SCM automation end to end. Python services, Streamlit workflows, front-end glue, whatever the job actually needs. Before that I was at HTX building threat-detection models for real checkpoint environments, which is a very efficient way to stop thinking in toy problems.

I'm also not great at leaving the deeper questions alone. So nights and weekends usually end up in Scripture, theology, and weird little tools for thinking more clearly about truth. Turns out the same brain that likes debugging models also likes debugging bad doctrine.

SUMMARY

I started with inherited belief and a lot of apologetics. Over time that turned into a much more personal search for truth, with Scripture first, Christ at the center, and enough honesty to admit some of my favorite frameworks were too small.

That meant wrestling seriously with sovereignty, free will, baptism, church life, and the kind of teaching that actually produces repentance, joy, unity, and love for Jesus instead of just making people smug.

The thing that has held steady is simple: salvation is by grace alone, through faith alone, in Jesus alone. If doctrine doesn't make us more honest, more humble, and more alive to God, something's off.
