The Story

How I got here — in engineering and in faith.

I first got drawn into AI watching systems like AlphaGo and AlphaStar. That curiosity led into computer science, then into production systems that have to hold up outside a clean notebook.

I am an AI Research Engineer at Panasonic (Singapore), owning internal supply-chain automation tools end to end. Before that I interned at HTX on checkpoint computer vision. Both roles rewarded the same habit: finish the last 20% that makes something actually usable.

Outside work I read Scripture and study theology. I do not treat faith as branding. I treat it as the claim that Jesus is Lord — which means my work, speech, and study should be answerable to something truer than my preferences.

SUMMARY

I started with inherited belief and a lot of apologetics. Over time, that became a more personal search for truth, with Scripture first, Christ at the center, and enough honesty to admit that some of my favorite frameworks were too small.

That meant wrestling seriously with sovereignty, free will, baptism, church life, and the kind of teaching that produces repentance, joy, unity, and love for Jesus.

The thing that has held steady is simple: salvation is by grace alone, through faith alone, in Jesus alone. If doctrine does not make us more honest, more humble, and more alive to God, something is off.
