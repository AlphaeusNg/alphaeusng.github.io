WHO I AM

The Story

I am an engineer who grew up captivated by systems that could outthink human masters — AlphaGo, AlphaStar, the elegant violence of perfect play in strategy games. That wonder led me into computer science and deep into the frontier of artificial intelligence.

Today I work as an AI Research Engineer at Panasonic, building computer vision systems that perceive and interpret the physical world. Previously at Singapore’s Home Team Science & Technology Agency, I developed models for threat detection deployed at our borders.

Yet the same mind that marvels at neural networks also hungers for something deeper. I am a created being who creates — and I spend my nights and weekends in Scripture, theology, and building tools to help others pursue truth with clarity and rigor.

“The created creating,
pursuing the Uncaused Cause.”

FAITH & CODE

Reflections

Why I build tools for truth-seeking

The same intellectual honesty that drives good engineering — precise definitions, falsifiability, humility before evidence — is desperately needed in how we approach Scripture and doctrine. I want to lower the barrier for careful, communal study while raising the standard of clarity.

The Logos and the model

John opens his Gospel with “In the beginning was the Word” — the Logos. The same Greek term from which we get logic, reason, and the study of computation. There is no contradiction between loving rigorous systems thinking and loving the One who spoke the universe into ordered existence.

“In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God.”

— John 1:1
