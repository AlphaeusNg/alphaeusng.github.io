WHO I AM

The Story

I am an engineer who grew up captivated by systems that could outthink human masters — AlphaGo, AlphaStar, the elegant violence of perfect play in strategy games. That wonder led me into computer science and deep into the frontier of artificial intelligence.

Today I work as an AI Research Engineer at Panasonic, building internal SCM automation end to end: Python services, Streamlit workflows, and the CSS, JavaScript, and HTML surfaces that make those systems usable in practice. Previously at Singapore’s Home Team Science & Technology Agency, I developed models for threat detection deployed at our borders.

Yet the same mind that marvels at neural networks also hungers for something deeper. I am a created being who creates — and I spend my nights and weekends in Scripture, theology, and building tools to help others pursue truth with clarity and rigor.

SUMMARY

My journey moved from inherited belief and apologetics into a much more personal search for truth: Scripture first, Christ at the center, and enough humility to be corrected when cherished frameworks were too small.

Over time that meant wrestling seriously with sovereignty, free will, baptism, church life, and what kind of teaching actually produces repentance, joy, unity, and love for Jesus rather than mere doctrinal defensiveness.

What remains most stable now is simple: salvation is by grace alone, through faith alone, in Jesus alone, and Christian maturity should make us more honest, more humble, and more alive to God rather than more proud of being right.
